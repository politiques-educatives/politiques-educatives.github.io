### A3. What academy freedoms meant (pre-2026 Act)
| Freedom | Rule |
|---|---|
| Curriculum | Not bound by the national curriculum. Must offer a "broad and balanced" curriculum under the funding agreement [S] |
| Pay and conditions | Not bound by the STPCD. Staff who transferred at conversion keep their terms under TUPE. Trusts can set their own terms for new hires (The Key; Edapt) [S] https://www.edapt.org.uk/support/knowledge-base/what-should-i-do-if-i-have-a-concern-with-my-teaching-timetable |
| Day / term length | Set by the trust. There is a non-statutory **32.5-hour minimum week** (breaks included) for all mainstream state schools from September 2024, enforced only through Ofsted (NASUWT; DfE note) [S] https://dera.ioe.ac.uk/39119/1/Minimum_expectation_for_length_of_school_week_-__information_note.pdf |
| Admissions | Trust is its own admissions authority but must follow the School Admissions Code via the funding agreement [S] |
| Unqualified teachers | Could hire without QTS |
| Contract | **Funding agreement** = a contract between the trust and the Secretary of State, funded directly by DfE (not via the local authority). Intervention runs through regional directors |

**Local authorities' remaining role** [S]: SEND/EHCPs, place planning (sufficiency duty), coordinated admissions, Fair Access Protocol, children missing education. They have no line management of academies.

### A4. Children's Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026: school provisions
| Provision | Content | Commencement |
|---|---|---|
| Pay **floor** | Academies must pay at least the STPCD minima and "have regard" to the document. They can pay above it, not below. Watered down from the original "follow the STPCD" | **1 Sept 2027** (Schools Week) |

### A6. Accountability
- **Attainment 8** = a pupil's points across 8 GCSE slots (English and maths double-weighted, 3 EBacc subjects, 3 open). **Progress 8** = Attainment 8 relative to pupils with the same KS2 prior attainment (value-added), published per school in league tables (performance tables) (EPI) [S]. https://epi.org.uk/publications-and-research/analysis-introduction-progress-8/
- **KS2 SATs** (age 10–11): reading and maths tests, teacher-assessed writing. 2025: **62%** met the expected standard in reading, writing and maths combined (61% in 2024); reading 75%, maths 74%, writing 72% [S, DfE release]. 2026: 63.3% [S]. https://explore-education-statistics.service.gov.uk/find-statistics/key-stage-2-attainment-national-headlines/2024-25

## B. Teacher working time and pay in England

### B1. STPCD working time (current: STPCD 2026, dated 14 Oct 2026, valid from 1 Sept 2026) [V]
https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6ad21656dfa45fcb6753cb0b/School_teachers_pay_and_conditions_document_and_guidance_2026.pdf
| Rule | Para. | Text |
|---|---|---|
| 195 days | 52.2 | 190 days teaching + other duties, 5 days other duties only (INSET). Days specified by the employer or the head |
| **1,265 h directed time** | 52.5 | "available to perform such duties at such times and such places as may be specified by the headteacher… for 1265 hours… allocated reasonably" |
| Extra hours | 52.7–52.8 | Must work "reasonable additional hours" for planning, marking etc. The employer "must not determine how many" or when |
| **PPA ≥10%** | 53.4 | PPA (planning, preparation and assessment) of at least 10% of timetabled teaching time, in units of at least 30 minutes, within the 1,265 h. It can be taken as one weekly block and off-site if the teacher and head agree |
| No fixed teaching load | — | No maximum teaching hours. The head sets the timetable within the 1,265 h, PPA, a "reasonable" midday break (53.3), rare cover (53.6) and work/life balance (53.4, Working Time Regulations 1998) |
| Weekends | 53.1 | No weekend or holiday work unless the contract says so, a genuine emergency, or volunteering |
| Scope | Intro | Applies to LA-maintained, foundation and VA schools. **Academies are not bound** (until the 2027 pay floor) |

In practice academies mostly follow the STPCD. 94% of trusts follow national pay and conditions (National School Trust Report, via LGA) [S]. Browne Jacobson 2023 trust survey: 99% applied the national award (about 100 respondents) [S]. https://brownejacobson.com/insights/school-staff-pay-survey-results-2023
DfE 2014 survey: 24% of academies had changed staff pay structures and 12% planned to (see E3) [V].

### B2. Workload policy
- **Workload Reduction Taskforce**, initial recommendations Jan 2024. The target was to cut teachers' and leaders' hours by **5 h/week within 3 years**. All but one recommendation accepted; an extra INSET day was rejected. A list of admin tasks teachers should not do was put back into the STPCD. Dropping PRP was also recommended (Schools Week, Tes) [S]. https://schoolsweek.co.uk/dfes-workload-reduction-taskforce-the-early-recommendations-in-full/
- **STPCD 2024**: PRP no longer mandatory. If a school drops it, progression can be withheld only under capability procedures. The PPA-at-home clarification was added (NASUWT) [S], and the 2026 text confirms both [V]. https://nasuwt.org.uk/advice/performance-management/changes-to-prp-from-2024-25-england.html
- **STPCD 2026**: more INSET flexibility, limits on leaders' weekend work (53.1), a new non-consolidated recognition reward [V].

### B4. Pay
- **STRB** (School Teachers' Review Body, independent) receives the DfE remit, takes evidence and recommends. The government accepts, rejects or modifies, then sets the award in the STPCD.
- 2024: +5.5%. **UNVERIFIED this session.** 2025: +4%, STRB 35th report, accepted in full [S]. 2026: multi-year deal, **+3.5% (Sept 2026), +3% (Sept 2027)**, indicative 3% for 2028/29. The unqualified minimum rises 5%. £1.8bn extra funding (STRB 36th report; DfE blog) [S]. https://educationhub.blog.gov.uk/2026/07/teacher-pay-everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-latest-multi-year-pay-award/

- **PRP**: mandatory since 2013 (STPCD 2013, pay progression linked to appraisal); optional since STPCD 2024 [S].

|---|---|---|

### D3. Evaluations of the 2013 reform

### D4. Territorial roles
- Communes own and maintain écoles, employ ATSEM and periscolaire staff, decide the 4-day derogation. Départements run collèges, régions run lycées.
- School transport: transferred from départements to **régions** by the loi NOTRe (loi 2015-991, 7 Aug 2015), effective 1 Sept 2017. **Not re-verified this session** (search budget exhausted).
- Implication: in France, changing the school week is a commune-transport-periscolaire decision. In England, the trust or head sets the day and term (A3).

## Summary: 10 most useful facts for a French note on ORS and autonomy
1. England has **no teaching-hours maximum**. The STPCD sets 195 days and **1,265 h of directed time** at the head's disposal, plus ≥10% PPA (STPCD 2026, §52.2, 52.5, 53.4). France fixes **18 h (certifiés) / 15 h (agrégés) / 24 h + 108 h (PE)** per week nationally.
2. OECD 2025: France's statutory teaching time is **900 / 720 / 720 h** (primary / lower / upper secondary) against an OECD average of **770 / 710 / 678**. England reports none; its actual teaching time is **893 / 777 / 777 h** over **190 days** (France 162 days in primary).
3. Real workload: English full-time teachers report **50.1 h/week** (2025, WLTL) and French full-time collège teachers **38.7 h** (TALIS 2024). England did **not** take part in TALIS 2024.
4. TALIS 2018: England full-time lower secondary taught 20.5 h, close to the OECD average of about 21.5. The gap was **32.7 h of non-teaching tasks against about 26 h OECD**. Teaching hours are not the difference; out-of-class load is.
5. **60.4% of English pupils** (84% at secondary, 50% at primary) attend academies (Jan 2026). They are funded directly by DfE under a contract, outside LA control.
6. Autonomy has been **partly re-centralised** by the Children's Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026: a pay floor (2027), the national curriculum, QTS (2027), LA admissions powers, and the end of automatic academisation and of the free-school presumption.
7. Freedoms were **little used for time**: only 8% of academies lengthened the day and 4% changed terms (DfE 2014). Most trusts still follow the national pay scales (about 94%).
8. Causal evidence: autonomy worked for **failing schools that gained the most autonomy plus a new head** (+0.2 SD, Eyles & Machin, JEEA 2019). It had **no effect for primary converters** (Eyles, Machin & McNally 2017), and differences between MATs exceed the gap between academies and LA schools.
9. France's 4-day week covers **93% of communes** (2023), despite the Cour des comptes (May 2025) judging it out of step with pupils' needs. The only causal study of the 2013 reform concerns **mothers' employment** (gender gap on Wednesday −15%, IPP 2017), not learning.
10. England pairs autonomy with **strong outcome accountability**: Progress 8 value-added, published tables, KS2 SATs (62% at the expected standard in 2025), now MAT-level tables, and Ofsted inspection of trusts from 2027-28. France has neither school-level value-added publication nor head control of service time.
