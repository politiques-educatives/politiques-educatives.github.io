## 1. Executive summary

1. **Verdict: moderate, mostly indirect evidence.** French data show the diploma works as a *first-stage screen* on CVs, not as the reason a candidate is finally chosen. No French study measures the share of junior offers that demand a bac+5 the job does not need.
2. Best French evidence (Dares, Ofer 2016): the diploma is the final selection criterion in only **11 %** of hires (**22 %** for cadres), but the survey reports it "peut servir de filtre pour trier les candidats". **73 %** of recruiters say they could have hired someone less qualified but more experienced.
3. Comparative evidence: in a nine-country recruiter experiment, French and German recruiters preferred master's graduates for junior posts; UK recruiters preferred bachelor's graduates (SECONDARY). EY-Parthenon says it hires "quasi exclusivement des masters" in France but opens UK and Luxembourg offices to bachelors (SECONDARY).
4. Against the filter story: French job ads ask for *fewer* diplomas over time (Indeed: no diploma stated in 61.7 % of 2024 ads, up from 53.1 % in 2017), **29 %** of private-sector cadres hold less than bac+3, and bac+5 still pays more than bac+3/4 (Céreq).
5. Reading: the filter works through the candidate pool (79 % of applicants to Apec cadre offers hold bac+5), not through written requirements. Students take a master's because everyone else does, a classic signalling race.
6. Most promising levers: professionalised licence with alternance (licence pro beats master on early employment), State as exemplary employer, outcome data per programme, and a recurring employer question "un diplômé moins élevé aurait-il convenu ?" that Ofer already asked once.
7. Generic hiring subsidies are weak: the 2020-21 AEJ raised youth hiring by about 8 % but cost more than 900 M€ with no clear effect on total youth employment.
8. Biggest gaps: a French job-ad study of junior bac+5 requirements, and any French evaluation of MRS or skills-based hiring outcomes.

## 2. Part 1: Evidence that French firms use bac+5 as a selection device

### 2.1 Job ads

- **VERIFIED. French ads require fewer diplomas, not more.** Indeed Hiring Lab France (Lisa Feist, 12 Nov 2024), all French Indeed postings, Jan-Sep each year: postings with no diploma requirement rose from **53.1 % (2017) to 61.7 % (2024)**; postings asking at least a licence fell from **16.4 % to 14.5 %**. The licence requirement fell in **83 %** of job families. Largest falls: insurance −12.7 points, marketing −6.9, HR −6.5, software development −5.9. Increases in regulated health jobs (pharmacy +9.1, nursing +7.1). Bac+5 not reported separately. https://hiringlab.indeed.com/fr/blog/2024/11/12/le-diplome-est-de-moins-en-moins-important-sur-le-marche-du-travail-en-france/
- **VERIFIED. Apec cadre offers, 2024.** **30 %** of cadre offers on apec.fr are open to young graduates (less than one year of experience). **79 %** of candidates to Apec cadre offers hold bac+5 or more. Among private-sector cadres in employment, **71 %** hold bac+3 or more (Insee Enquêtes Emploi 2023-2024, cited by Apec), so **29 %** hold less. Apec, *Métiers verts et emploi cadre*, Dec 2025, p. 3-4. https://corporate.apec.fr/files/live/sites/corporate/files/Nos%20etudes/PDF/metiers%20verts%20et%20emploi%20cadre%202025.pdf . (A search snippet claimed "54 % of private-sector cadres hold bac+5": **not found** in the text; do not use.)
- **NOT FOUND.** Share of "jeune diplômé" or "débutant accepté" offers requiring bac+5 (Apec, France Travail, HelloWork, Jobteaser, Welcome to the Jungle), and any French "diploma required vs diploma held" comparison by occupation. The companion file reached the same result.
- **Reading.** The French filter is not written in ads. It operates through who applies and who gets shortlisted: when 79 % of applicants hold bac+5, a recruiter can screen on it without stating it.

### 2.2 Recruiter practice and surveys

- **VERIFIED. Diploma used to sort, rarely to choose.** Pôle emploi, *Éclairages et synthèses* n° 43, March 2018, on Dares Ofer 2016 (establishments that recruited Sept-Nov 2015). In a CV, **74 %** of employers look first at experience in the trade and **57 %** at behavioural skills; **40 %** look first at diplomas, more in large establishments. The diploma is cited as a reason for the final choice in **11 %** of hires: **22 %** for cadres, **17 %** for professions intermédiaires, **3 %** for low-skilled workers. Quote: the diploma "peut servir de filtre pour trier les candidats dans un premier temps"; **60 %** of employers call it important for cadre and intermediate posts. https://www.francetravail.org/files/live/sites/peorg/files/documents/Statistiques-et-analyses/E%26S/ES_43_comment_les_employeurs_selectionent_les_candidats_qu'ils_retiennent_dans_leur_recrutement.pdf
- **VERIFIED. Recruiters admit a less qualified hire would have done.** Lhommeau and Rémy, *Économie et Statistique* n° 534-35, 2022, Ofer 2016, Table 4: **35 %** of recruiters had no diploma expectation; **73 %** say they "aurai[en]t pu embaucher quelqu'un de moins diplômé mais de plus expérimenté" (**71 %** in technical occupations, the class where 64 % of hires hold bac+2 or more). Caveat: all hires, not cadres débutants; the trade-off is diploma against experience, which young graduates lack. https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/fichier/6530515/04_ES534-35_Lhommeau-Remy_FR.pdf
- **SECONDARY.** Dares Analyses n° 10, Feb 2022, "Comment les employeurs recrutent-ils un jeune candidat ?" (Ofer 2016): for juniors, dynamism, presentation and being operational come first; "plus le niveau d'études est élevé, plus le diplôme est déterminant". Dares site blocked by captcha; via Lefebvre Dalloz and BnF notice. https://dares.travail-emploi.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/8f21e540868fb8b28f62420de04cfc74/DA_recrutement_jeunes.pdf
- **SECONDARY. Céreq, upgrading of intermediate jobs.** Cadet, Delanoë, Guitton, Céreq Bref n° 397, 2020: entry into professions intermédiaires (historically bac+2) is now mostly at bac+3; firms use the diploma as a guarantee of general capacities and teach job skills in-house; share of higher-education graduates in these jobs 48.5 % in 2017. Read via an AEF summary only (Céreq and HAL blocked). https://halshs.archives-ouvertes.fr/halshs-03045526v1/file/cadet_bref397_2020.pdf
- **SECONDARY. School name.** L'Etudiant poll of 200 recruiters (undated): only **4 %** attach much importance to the school or university name. https://www.letudiant.fr/jobsstages/lettres-de-motivation_1/sondage-exclusif-ce-que-les-recruteurs-regardent-le-plus-sur-les-cv-17376.html . No source found for published "école cible" lists (**NOT FOUND**).
- **NOT FOUND.** An Ofer wave after 2016; Institut Montaigne, CAE or France Stratégie notes specifically on bac+5 as a hiring filter.

### 2.3 Academic evidence on signalling and credentialism

- **SECONDARY. France prefers masters, the UK bachelors.** Humburg and van der Velden, "Skills and the graduate recruitment process", *Economics of Education Review* 49 (2015): vignette experiments with recruiters in nine European countries for a typical junior graduate post. On average bachelor's and master's graduates were treated equally, but "recruiters in the UK [had] a preference for bachelor graduates and recruiters in several other countries, such as France and Germany, ... for master graduates". Quoted from Verhaest et al. (below); original not read (PDF download failed). https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:ecoedu:v:49:y:2015:i:c:p:24-41
- **VERIFIED (Belgium, not France). Employers reward over-qualification.** Verhaest, Bogaert, Dereymaeker, Mestdagh, Baert, "Do employers prefer overqualified graduates? A field experiment", *Industrial Relations*, 2018: paired fictitious applications to real Belgian vacancies requiring a bachelor's. Master's applicants were **19 %** more likely to be invited to interview and **11 %** more likely to get any positive reply, more so in shortage occupations. This is the mechanism behind a degree race: when a master's beats a bachelor's for bachelor-level jobs, students rationally continue. https://lirias.kuleuven.be/retrieve/c0c04b1f-6299-4b9f-9223-1063f8187b45
- **SECONDARY. Crowding-out down the ladder.** Insee (*Le non-emploi des peu ou pas diplômés en France et en Europe : un effet classement du diplôme*, 2020): a person's rank in the diploma distribution predicts non-employment; through the déclassement of graduates, the least qualified were partly pushed out of work. Snippet only. https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/4501168
- **VERIFIED (bibliographic) / SECONDARY (content).** Marie Duru-Bellat, *L'inflation scolaire. Les désillusions de la méritocratie*, Seuil, La République des idées, 2006: argues accumulated credentials lose value as everyone acquires them. https://www.mollat.com/livres/57175/marie-duru-bellat-l-inflation-scolaire-les-desillusions-de-la-meritocratie
- **NOT FOUND.** A French correspondence test comparing bac+3 and bac+5 (or university and grande école) applicants for the same junior job. The TEPP/ONDES "MASTER" tests concern access *to* master's programmes, not to jobs.

### 2.4 Sector cases

- **SECONDARY. Strategy consulting.** Consultor, 15 Dec 2025, reporting Jérôme Fabry (partner, EY-Parthenon): "en France, EY-Parthenon recrute quasi exclusivement des masters"; the opening of UK and Luxembourg offices "aux « bachelors » ou BBA" shows "qu'il ne s'agit pas d'une contrainte métier, mais d'un choix culturel". https://www.consultor.fr/articles/vers-la-fin-de-la-prime-au-bac-5-y-compris-dans-le-conseil-en-strategie
- **SECONDARY. Audit (Big Four France).** Junior ads from KPMG and PwC France ask for bac+5 (business school, engineering school or university); bac+3 entry exists mainly as alternance (DCG holders going on to DSCG). Aggregator ads and older recruitment brochures, not a study. https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/pdf/2016/06/fr-recrutement-plaquette.pdf
- **SECONDARY. Public sector.** The external concours for attaché d'administration de l'État (first category A grade) requires a licence (level 6), not a master's (digiSchool, IRA brochures). https://www.digischool.fr/articles/concours/concours-attache-administration-etat-2027/ . Teacher concours moved to L3 in 2026: see companion file.
- **SECONDARY. Recruitment without CV or diploma.** France Travail's MRS (méthode de recrutement par simulation) excludes CV, diploma and experience; candidates who pass job simulations go to interview. Evidence is testimony only (e.g. candidate pools "multipliés par trois, voire quatre"). The one evaluation found (2010) could not be opened. Used mostly for volume and shortage jobs, not cadre posts. https://www.francetravail.fr/employeur/vos-recrutements/selectionnez-des-candidats/la-methode-de-recrutement-par-si.html
- **NOT FOUND.** Sourced, quantified French corporate programmes hiring licence holders into roles usually given to bac+5 (La Poste, Orange, L'Oréal, Accor were searched; nothing usable).

### 2.5 Counter-evidence

- **VERIFIED (companion file).** Bac+5 still beats bac+3/4 three years out: employment 87 % vs 78 %, median first pay 1,810 € vs 1,530 € (Céreq Génération 2021). Céreq rejects the diploma-inflation hypothesis at bac+5.
- **UNVERIFIED (not checked this session).** Regulated professions (medicine, pharmacy, law via the bar school, chartered accountancy) and the engineering title genuinely require bac+5 or more.
- **Interpretation.** A real premium is compatible with a filter: if firms screen on bac+5, holders earn more whether or not the job needs it. Only a test of the same job filled at bac+3 vs bac+5 would separate the two, and none exists for France.

## 4. Usable in the campaign note

1. « Le diplôme sert d'abord à trier les CV : il ne décide du choix final que dans 11 % des recrutements, et dans 22 % pour les cadres (Dares, enquête Ofer 2016). »
2. « 73 % des recruteurs reconnaissent qu'ils auraient pu embaucher une personne moins diplômée mais plus expérimentée (Dares, Ofer 2016). »
3. « Au Royaume-Uni, les recruteurs préfèrent un bachelor pour un premier poste ; en France, ils préfèrent un master (Humburg et van der Velden, 2015, à vérifier dans l'original). »
4. « Un an et demi après le diplôme, 81 % des diplômés de licence professionnelle sont salariés, contre 73 % des diplômés de master et 55 % des licences générales (Insee, InserSup, 2025). »
5. « Seuls 30 % des étudiants obtiennent leur licence en trois ans (SIES, 2025) : raccourcir le chemin vers l'emploi commence là. »
6. « La prime à l'embauche des jeunes de 2020 a coûté plus de 900 millions d'euros sans effet net sur l'emploi total des jeunes (Dares, Cour des comptes, à vérifier) : il faut cibler, pas arroser. »
